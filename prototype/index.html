FIRST-SLICE ASSUMPTIONS

How to read this study

The heatmap estimates direct SPL from the peak rating at 1 metre, output offset, distance, and a smooth generic HF pattern. Nominal horizontal and vertical beamwidths are −6 dB in their respective planes.

Speaker height means the top of the cabinet above the common room-floor reference. For this first slice, that top point is also the modeled sound source. Sound level is capped at its 1-metre reference inside 1 metre.

Speakers combine by adding energies. The overlap advisory marks points where the two strongest contributors are within 6 dB and both are inside their nominal HF zones. It does not predict cancellation.

Seating is a continuous ramp. Tables and the stage are layout objects. Room reflections, obstructions, frequency-dependent measurements, and phase/delay interactions are not modeled.

Use this skeleton to explore layout and interaction. The generic directional model is not a manufacturer-calibrated prediction.
